Featured Project
AI Usage and Student Academic Performance
Research Question: How is daily AI usage associated with students' study hours, critical thinking scores, assignment scores, and GPA in a synthetic dataset?

The analysis shows a nonlinear relationship between AI usage and GPA. GPA increases slightly at lower to moderate levels of AI usage but decreases at higher levels. The correlation analysis also shows negative associations between AI usage, critical thinking, and GPA.

The dataset is synthetic, so the results do not represent real students and cannot establish causation.
